Learning Objectives / Main Outcomes
Learners will achieve the lesson target through age-appropriate listening, speaking, reading and/or writing activities and demonstrate evidence of learning.
5E StageTeacher Role + SirajLearner Role + SirajTime
1. EngageActivate prior knowledge using a question, visual, object, song or short classroom situation. Use Siraj for a motivating prompt and quick diagnostic check.Respond, predict, recall prior learning and connect the topic to personal experience. Interact with Siraj as a learning partner.5 min
2. ExploreGuide learners through the relevant Pupil’s Book / Activity Book task. Use Siraj to provide modelling, visuals, audio or guided practice.Explore, observe, listen, match, sort, read or practise individually, in pairs or groups with Siraj support.10 min
3. ExplainElicit and clarify meaning, pronunciation, vocabulary, grammar or language function. Model accurate English and use Siraj for examples and immediate feedback.Explain what they notice, answer checking questions, practise the target language and respond to Siraj feedback.10 min
4. ElaborateSet a meaningful communicative, creative or problem-solving task. Differentiate support and challenge using Siraj where appropriate.Apply the language in a new context, communicate, create, solve, role-play or retell with peers and Siraj.15 min
5. EvaluateCheck achievement of the objective through an exit task, performance, oral response or short written evidence. Use Siraj for self-checking and feedback.Demonstrate learning, complete the exit check, self-assess and state what they learned.5 min
